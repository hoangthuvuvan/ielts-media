Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

IELTS Factory Publisher is used only by the owner of the IELTS Factory accounts to publish their own content.

Data we access

When the account owner connects TikTok or Facebook, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (display name, open ID) needed to upload and publish posts to that account.

How we use it

Tokens are stored privately in the owner's Google Apps Script project and are used only to upload the owner's own videos and images and to check the status of those posts. We do not sell, share or use the data for advertising or any other purpose.

Retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect at any time by revoking access in TikTok or Facebook settings; stored tokens are then deleted from the project.
